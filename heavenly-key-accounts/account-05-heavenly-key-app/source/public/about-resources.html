About Our Bible Resources

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement provides Bible reading, Bible Champions, and hymn resources to encourage the study of God’s Word, Christian worship and spiritual growth.

Scripture and study references

Bible reading and Scripture references use the Authorised King James Version (KJV). Explanatory summaries are study aids, not substitutes for reading Scripture in its full context.

Hymns and audio

Hymn texts and recordings may have different authors, performers and rights holders. We respect their rights and preserve any source-specific credits and licence notices required for individual works.
