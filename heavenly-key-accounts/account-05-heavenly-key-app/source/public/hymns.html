Acknowledgement & Appreciation

This Hymn Library is a collection of timeless Christian hymns sung and cherished by different Bible-believing churches and ministries across the world, presented to glorify God, strengthen believers, inspire revival, and bless the entire Body of Christ.

We gratefully acknowledge the contributions of different Bible-believing churches, hymn writers, composers, choirs, and music ministries who have preserved these sacred songs and passed them down through generations.

These hymns are shared in the spirit of Christian love, unity, and fellowship, giving glory to God and honouring the rich spiritual heritage of the Christian faith.

We respect the original creators, performers, and rights holders of these hymns and recordings.
