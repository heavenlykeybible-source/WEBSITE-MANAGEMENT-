REVIVAL • HOLINESS • POWER

This hymn book brings together treasured Christian hymns from different generations of gospel hymn writers. We appreciate the dedication and contributions of Christian hymn writers, gospel musicians and Bible-believing churches whose music ministries have helped proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and strengthen believers.

This free, non-commercial hymn collection is provided for the edification of the Body of Christ, spiritual growth, biblical worship, revival, holiness, evangelism and soul-winning. We acknowledge the original hymn writers and composers wherever known. The historical texts in this collection are intended to be public-domain works; modern arrangements, translations and recordings may carry separate rights.

Please share this hymn book with family, friends, churches and Christian fellowships to encourage faith and spread the Gospel.
