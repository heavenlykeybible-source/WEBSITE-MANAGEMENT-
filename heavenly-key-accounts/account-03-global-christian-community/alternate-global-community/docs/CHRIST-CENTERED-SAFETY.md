# Christ-centered community care

The community exists to advance the gospel of Christ, equip the saints, uphold the faith of our fathers, fulfill destiny, and encourage meaningful, purposeful lives in God's will.

Member reports route to active site administrators, owners and moderators. Reports about group posts, replies or the group itself also route to active, approved group owners, administrators and moderators. Reporter identity is omitted from the group queue. Group review records its own note and can remove group posts or replies while leaving site review open. Only site staff can decide restrictions on the group itself. Site decisions retain their existing independent appeal process; members can also submit a Safety concern about group decisions.

Automated checks run when text is submitted. Clear prohibited text is blocked; ambiguous religious debate, suspicious solicitation and targeted insults are held for context review. Sensitive recovery testimony is held instead of treated as promotional pornography. Administrators and moderators can review flags, read held text, and approve or remove held posts, replies and messages. Excerpts of blocked attempts remain in the review queue. Existing historic flags without a reliable content type can be acknowledged but cannot restore content. Repeated flags are counted over seven days as a review aid, not proof of wrongdoing.

Only a site administrator or owner can lock a member account after supplying a reason. Locks block community API access, posting and messaging, including existing sessions. Members can still sign in, read their restriction reason and send a Safety concern requesting review. Administrators can unlock accounts. Staff accounts are excluded from this member control. Audit records and reasons are retained. No keyword or report count automatically locks an account.

Radio and TV links use the ministry's verified hosted /pages/radio and /pages/video destinations at heavenlykey.alldaywow247.workers.dev in a new browser tab with noopener/noreferrer. The community tab remains open. There is no autoplay, embedded stream or guaranteed mobile background playback. Stream availability depends on the ministry's broadcast service; the TV page had no programme available during verification. The older heavenlykey.org/pages/radio.html destination returned 404 and is not used.

Strength for today provides prepared, Scripture-referenced reflections by theme and a daily rotation, with a copy control. It makes no AI or prophecy claim and uses no model API or background polling.

Limits: these are rule-based text checks, not a comprehensive AI classifier. They cannot reliably understand all contexts or languages, detect every evasion, scan linked videos or prove a member's intention. Notifications are in-app, not email or push. Queue limits are 100 flags/group reports and 200 site reports. Operators must assign real staff accounts, review queues regularly and maintain ministry broadcast destinations.

Validation covers database migrations, duplicate report routing, report privacy, unauthorized group review, separate group/site decisions, held testimony approval, account role protections, current-session locks, restricted sign-in and review requests, restoration, and existing feed/messaging/group/registration behavior.
