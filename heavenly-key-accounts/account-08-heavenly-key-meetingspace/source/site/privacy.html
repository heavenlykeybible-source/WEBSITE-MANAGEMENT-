Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This policy explains how [BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") handles personal data in Live Studio. We are the data controller. Address: [ADDRESS]. Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL]. Data protection officer or contact person: [NAME AND EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, a securely hashed password, the date you joined, whether you confirmed your email, your plan end date, and the date and version of the Terms you accepted.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Paystack. We do not see or store your card details. We keep the payment reference, plan, amount and date.
  • Connected channels: if you connect YouTube channels or Facebook Pages, we store the channel or Page name and an access token. Tokens are encrypted. We use them only to create and end broadcasts that you start.
  • Guests and rooms: when someone joins a guest room we handle their name, their video and audio, their chat messages and their network address. Video and audio travel directly between browsers where possible. Chat passes through our server and is not stored.
  • Groups, meetings and follow-up: if you use groups we store the group name, your display name and role, your membership date, the messages in the group, and which meetings you joined (and whether it was your first). Group leaders and admins can see members' email addresses and meeting attendance, and can send follow-up messages to members in the group chat and, if they choose, by email. Other members see only names.
  • Programs: a program page you create (title, description, date, watch link) is public to anyone who has its link.
  • Your broadcast: the stream you send is processed by our server and forwarded to the destinations you choose. We do not store it. Recordings and photos you make stay in your own browser.
  • AI features: text you send to the announcer voice or the AI writer is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce the result. Please do not enter sensitive personal data.
  • Security and technical data: your network address and email are used in short-lived counters to limit abuse. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the service and take payment (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To meet accounting, tax and legal duties (legal obligation).
  • To send service emails such as email confirmation and password reset (performance of a contract). We do not send marketing email unless you agree. [CONFIRM]
  • Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Other services your browser contacts

Some features load code or content directly from other providers, who can see your network address when they do: jsDelivr (the background-blur model and the strong noise-removal code), Google (YouTube viewer embeds when an audience watch link is a YouTube link), and the sharing sites you choose to open. Strong noise removal runs on your own device. Your voice is not sent to a third party for it.

Who we share it with

Service providers who process data for us: Cloudflare (hosting, storage, AI), Paystack (payments), Resend (email), [YOUR STREAMING SERVER HOST]. If you connect a platform, we also exchange data with Google (YouTube) or Meta (Facebook) under their terms. Broadcast destinations you choose receive your stream. We do not sell personal data. Some providers are outside Nigeria, so data may be transferred internationally. [CONFIRM SAFEGUARDS WITH EACH PROVIDER]

Guests and what hosts do

A host who invites guests decides whether to broadcast or record them. The host is responsible for telling guests and for obtaining any consent the law requires. Guests are shown a notice before joining. The host can see a guest's network address as part of how direct connections work.

How long we keep it

We keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can. Most of it deletes itself.

  • Video and audio: we do not store them. Recordings, photos and share pictures stay on your own device.
  • Room chat, raised hands and reactions: not stored. They exist only while the room is open.
  • Group messages: deleted automatically after the time the group leader chooses (24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, 30 by default). Each group also keeps at most its latest 500 messages. Anyone who saw a message may have copied it, and we cannot delete copies.
  • Meeting links: erased when the meeting ends, or within a day if it was never ended.
  • Meeting attendance records: deleted after 90 days.
  • Invite links: expire after 7 days and are then deleted.
  • Program pages: removed 7 days after the program date, or 90 days after creation if there is no date.
  • Connected YouTube or Facebook access: deleted when you disconnect or delete your account. Saved access is not removed just because it has not been used recently.
  • Account data: kept until you delete your account. We also delete an account that is never confirmed by email after 14 days, and an account with no connected channels, no plan and no sign-in for 12 months (we email a warning about 30 days before when email is set up). Groups led by a deleted account are deleted.
  • Payment and accounting records: [NUMBER] years, as required by law. [CONFIRM]
  • Abuse-limit counters: from one hour to two days.

When you delete your account your name on past group messages is replaced with "Deleted user" and your memberships and attendance records are removed. [CONFIRM]

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. In the Account tab you can download your data and delete your account yourself. Deleting your account removes your login, your plan record and your connected channels, and revokes our YouTube access where possible. Facebook access can be removed in your Facebook settings under Business Integrations. Payment records we must keep are not deleted. To use any other right, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Channel tokens are encrypted. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the regulator within the time the law requires. [CONFIRM PROCESS]

Children

Live Studio is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Hosts must not invite children to a room without a parent or guardian's permission.

Cookies and local storage

We keep you signed in using your browser's local storage. A short-lived security cookie is used while you connect a YouTube or Facebook account. We do not use advertising cookies.
