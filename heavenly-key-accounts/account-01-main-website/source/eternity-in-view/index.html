The Eternal Question

Life is brief. God's invitation is for today.

Read, reflect on Scripture, and consider your response to Jesus Christ.

01

Empty We Came, Empty We Will All Leave

Genesis 3:19 · Job 1:21 · Mark 8:36

We arrived with nothing, and we will take none of our possessions beyond this life. Wealth, success, influence and fame are temporary. Scripture reminds us that we return to dust. What shall it profit anyone to gain the whole world and lose their soul? Turn to Jesus today; His grace offers forgiveness and hope beyond this passing world.

I SAY YES TO JESUS →
02

Humanity Truly Needs God

Acts 17:28 · Proverbs 10:22

Technology, science, and wealth can accomplish remarkable things, yet humanity remains dependent on God for life itself. Disasters remind us how fragile our achievements are. Like a child forgetting the parent who nurtured them, we can forget the source of our strength. Do not exchange holiness for quick gain. Refuse corruption even when others compromise. Receive God's gifts with gratitude, patience, and a heart committed to what is right.

I SAY YES TO JESUS →
03

Revival of Repentance, Salvation and Holiness

Acts 3:19 · Hebrews 12:14

True revival is more than a gathering or moment of emotion. It begins when hearts turn from sin toward Jesus Christ. Repentance bears fruit in mercy, honesty, obedience and holy living. Come to Christ, receive His forgiveness, and follow Him faithfully.

I SAY YES TO JESUS →
04

Knowing God for Yourself and Understanding the Scriptures Is Now

Daniel 11:32 · 2 Timothy 2:15 · Acts 17:11

Do not depend only on the faith of your parents, pastor, church or denomination. Seek a personal relationship with God through Jesus Christ. Pray, study Scripture, and carefully examine teachings in the light of God's Word. Those who know their God can stand firm when misleading voices arise.

I SAY YES TO JESUS →
05

What Are Wealth, Technology, High Position and the Good Things of Life Without Jesus?

Mark 8:36 · Matthew 6:33

Wealth and knowledge can be used for good, but they cannot replace peace with God. What value is worldly achievement if we neglect our souls? Seek Christ first, then use every opportunity and possession to love others and honor Him.

I SAY YES TO JESUS →
06

Until You Make Peace with Jesus

Romans 5:1 · Ephesians 2:8–9

No title, status or reputation can earn reconciliation with God. Through faith in Jesus Christ, we are invited to receive grace, forgiveness and a new purpose. Your life matters. Respond to His invitation with faith and repentance today.

Call to Salvation Now

I SAY YES TO JESUS

Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). Scripture calls us to repent and believe the gospel (Mark 1:15). Salvation is God's gift of grace through faith (Ephesians 2:8–9). You can turn to Him today.

A suggested prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, I acknowledge my sins and my need for Your mercy. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. Forgive me, lead me in repentance, and teach me to follow You in faith and holiness. Amen.

Prayer is not a magic formula. Trust in Christ, read the Bible, pray, connect with a faithful Christian fellowship, and grow in love and obedience.
