Statement of Faith — What We Believe

The Holy Bible is our final authority for faith and conduct.

Earnestly contending for the faith once delivered unto the saints (Jude 3).

This statement reproduces the 22 article summaries preserved in the All Nations for Christ Outreach website's About page. Scripture references are shown with each article.

1. The Holy Bible

That the Holy Bible, consisting of 39 books of the Old Testament and 27 books of the New Testament, is the inspired Word of God. We take the Bible as final authority in all matters concerning Christian conduct and work. (2 Timothy 3:16,17; Proverbs 30:5,6; Revelation 22:18,19.)

2. The Godhead

That the Godhead consists of three separate, distinct, and recognisable personalities and qualities, perfectly united in one. The Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost are different Persons in the Godhead. (Matthew 3:16,17; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Matthew 28:19,20.)

3. The Virgin Birth of Jesus

The virgin birth of Jesus, the only begotten Son of God, as well as His crucifixion, death, burial and bodily resurrection. (Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:18-25; Romans 1:4; I Corinthians 15:3,4.)

4. Total Depravity, Sinfulness & Guilt

The total depravity, sinfulness and guilt of all men since the Fall, rendering them subject to God's wrath and condemnation. (Psalm 51:5; Job 14:4; Romans 3:23; 5:12-17; Mark 7:21-23; Ephesians 2:1.)

5. Repentance

That Repentance is a complete turning away from all sin and its deceitful pleasures, and is required from every sinner before he can truly and effectively believe in Jesus with saving faith. (Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 55:7; Mark 1:15; Acts 2:38; 2 Corinthians 7:10.)

6. Restitution

That Restitution is making amends for wrongs done against our fellow men — restoring stolen things to their rightful owners, paying debts, giving back where one has defrauded. (Exodus 22:1-7; Leviticus 6:1-7.)

7. Justification

That Justification is God's grace through which one receives forgiveness and remission of sins and is counted righteous before God, through faith in the atoning blood of Jesus. (Psalm 32:1,2; Isaiah 1:18; Acts 13:38.)

8. Water Baptism

That water Baptism is essential to our obedience after reconciliation with God — one immersion "In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15,16; Romans 6:4,5.)

9. The Lord's Supper

That the Lord's Supper was instituted by Jesus Christ so that all believers might partake thereof regularly, to "shew the Lord's death till he come." (Matthew 26:29; I Corinthians 11:23-30.)

10. Entire Sanctification

That Entire Sanctification is a definite act of God's grace, subsequent to the New Birth, by which the believer's heart is purified and made holy. (I Thessalonians 4:3,7,8; 5:22-24.)

11. Holy Ghost Baptism

That the Baptism in the Holy Ghost is the enduement of power from on High upon the sanctified believer, accompanied by the initial evidence of speaking in tongues. (Matthew 3:11; Acts 1:8; Luke 24:49.)

12. Redemption, Healing and Health

That Redemption from the curse of the law, healing of sickness and disease, as well as continued health, are provided for all people through the sacrificial death of Jesus Christ. (Exodus 15:26; Psalm 103:1-5.)

13. Personal Evangelism

That Personal Evangelism is a God-given and God-ordained ministry for every believer. Jesus commanded every believer to be a compassionate and fruitful soul-winner. (Matthew 28:19,20; Mark 16:15; Acts 1:8.)

14. Marriage

That Marriage is binding for life. Monogamy is the uniform teaching of the Bible. Polygamy is contrary to God's perfect will and institution. (Genesis 2:24; 2 Corinthians 6:14-18.)

15. The Rapture

That the Rapture is the catching away from the earth of all living saints and all who died in the Lord, taking place before the Great Tribulation. (John 14:1-3; I Corinthians 15:51-58.)

16. The Resurrection of the Dead

That the Resurrection of the dead is taught in the Bible as clearly as the immortality of the soul. Every individual will be resurrected, some to honour and glory and others to everlasting shame. (Daniel 12:2; John 5:28,29.)

17. The Great Tribulation

That the Great Tribulation will occur after the Rapture and will be a time of terrible suffering on earth, also referred to as the time of "Jacob's trouble." (Matthew 24:21,22,29; 2 Thessalonians 2:3-12.)

18. The Second Coming of Christ

That the Second Coming of Christ will be just as literal and visible as His going away, and He is coming to execute judgement upon the ungodly. (Zechariah 14:3,4; Matthew 25:31-46.)

19. Christ's Millennial Reign

That Christ's Millennial Reign is the 1,000-year literal reign of Jesus on earth, ushered in by His coming back to earth with ten thousands of His saints. (Jude 14,15; Revelation 20:2,3.)

20. The Great White Throne Judgement

That the Great White Throne Judgement is when God finally judges all — the living and the dead, small and great — according to their works, after the Millennium. (John 5:24; Daniel 12:2,3.)

21. The New Heaven and the New Earth

That the New Heaven and the New Earth "wherein dwelleth righteousness" will be made by God, and the redeemed shall dwell therein with God forever. (Isaiah 51:6; 65:17.)

22. Hell

That Hell fire is a place of everlasting punishment where sinners (all who do not have their names in the Book of Life) will suffer torment for ever and ever. (Matthew 25:46; Luke 16:19-31.)
