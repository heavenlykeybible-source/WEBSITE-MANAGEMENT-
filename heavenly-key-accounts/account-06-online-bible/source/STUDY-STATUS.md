# Study tools

Implemented: device-local notes and saved verses; duplicate-safe updates; saved-entry removal; navigation to saved chapters; hide/reveal memorization; Knowing Jesus and Prayer and Faith seven-day plans; a 365-day whole-Bible plan; saved progress; downloadable study backups; import with source-reference verification and canonical KJV text reconstruction; current-note preservation on duplicate imports; cross-tab view refresh; hymn/gospel saved-title controls.

Backups identify KJV. Backups marked with another translation are rejected. Limits are 500 saved verses, 5,000 characters per note and 4 MB per import file. Imported text is never trusted as Scripture. Notes are saved locally; imports and exports let users move their study between devices without cloud accounts. They do not constitute cloud synchronization.

The six local test suites pass, along with JavaScript syntax checks and Wrangler 4.145.0 deployment dry run. Live browser/device testing and publication remain pending, with deployment deliberately deferred at the owner's request.
