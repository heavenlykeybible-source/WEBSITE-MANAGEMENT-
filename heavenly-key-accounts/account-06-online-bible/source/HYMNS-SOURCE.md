# Classic hymn text sources

The bundled catalogue contains historical public-domain lyrics only, not recordings or modern arrangements.

- Amazing Grace — John Newton; the six-stanza text in Olney Hymns (1840), Book 1, Hymn 41, transcribed at https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Olney_Hymns_(1840)/Book_1/Hymn_41. This preserves Newton's six stanzas rather than adding a later stanza.
- Blessed Assurance — Fanny J. Crosby, 1873; text from International Song Service (1895), hymn 73: https://hymnary.org/hymn/ISS1895/73. The source explicitly labels text and tune public domain.
- Holy, Holy, Holy — Reginald Heber; text from Hymnal for Church and Home, second edition (1928), hymn 3: https://hymnary.org/hymn/HFCH1928/3. The source explicitly labels the text public domain.

Source links and authors are included in the user-facing catalogue. Lyrics are preserved, including historical spelling and punctuation. Audio/video recording rights are separate and must follow the existing ministry-approved media workflow.
