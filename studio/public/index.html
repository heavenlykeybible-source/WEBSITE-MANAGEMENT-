PREPARE · PREVIEW · GO LIVE

Live Studio

Choose your source and programme title. Prepare a private preview, check the sound, then press Go live when you are ready.

What do the Studio controls mean?
Source
Your camera, microphone, a saved recording or an external stream.
Preview
A private check of your picture and sound before broadcasting.
Programme output
The picture and mixed sound you send to your audience.
Take over 24/7
Your live programme temporarily replaces automatic playback on the selected channel.
End broadcast
Ends your live programme. Scheduled or enabled 24/7 playback can resume.
Settings
Change sound, backgrounds, text, screen size, recording and saved preferences.

You can move between these menu sections while preparing. Use End broadcast to stop a live programme and Close preview to release your microphone or camera.

FAITH OF OUR FATHERS · REVIVAL RADIO

Your radio workspace

Speak live, add recorded messages or manage automatic playback.

Checking radio status…

Programme status appears here after sign-in.

1. Speak live

Use your microphone with the camera off. Enter a title, prepare your audio preview and check the microphone meter before going live.

2. Play recorded messages

Upload an audio recording privately. Preview it, then add it to the radio playlist.

3. Manage 24/7 radio

Enable automatic playback to use published audio when no live or scheduled programme has priority. Switching this off does not end a live broadcast.

Check what listeners hear

The status above describes the server programme. Open the listener page to confirm sound and playback on your device. Use headphones during a live programme to prevent feedback.

Open the radio listener page ↗
How live radio and 24/7 playback work together

Live radio takes priority on the radio channel. When it ends, a current scheduled programme takes priority, followed by enabled 24/7 playback. Publishing a recording adds it to automatic playback; it does not start a microphone broadcast.

SIMPLE FEATURE GUIDE

Help, one feature at a time

Open the explanation you need.

Home · choose your next task

Your starting point. Open Live Studio, Radio, Media Library or Connections using a large task card or the top menu.

Live Studio · prepare and broadcast

Choose a source, enter the programme title and prepare the preview. Check sound and the destination. Press Go live when ready. Press End broadcast when finished; Close preview releases your camera and microphone.

Radio · microphone programmes and recorded messages

Choose Prepare a radio programme for microphone-only broadcasting. For recorded messages, open audio recordings, upload a file and preview it before adding it to the radio playlist.

Media Library · private uploads and publishing

Upload audio or video up to 2 GB. An uploaded file is private until you publish it or use it in a programme. Pause stops after the current upload chunk. To resume, keep the same tab and select the same original file again. Temporary files normally expire after 48 hours; choose Keep permanently to retain a private upload. Publishing retains the file.

Connections · broadcast service and social platforms

LiveKit carries your live sound and video. Check the saved connection before replacing any details. For a social platform, prepare an event there, then save its RTMP server and stream key here. Choose destinations in the Studio final check and confirm the broadcast on each platform.

Studio settings · sound, background and recording

Output sets screen size and channel. Camera & Background controls your source and backdrop. Audio adjusts microphone, media and music levels. Branding adds programme text. Recording saves local clips; download or save them before leaving. Advanced stores your preferences for later.

Sign-in and returning to the website

Open Studio from your signed-in control room without entering another password. Use Renew sign-in if the session expires. Go back to the main website opens the ministry website in a separate tab so this workspace stays open.
