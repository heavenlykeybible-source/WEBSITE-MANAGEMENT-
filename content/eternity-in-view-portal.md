# ETERNITY IN VIEW PORTAL
**Where will you spend eternity? A life beyond planet Earth?**

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement — Revival • Holiness • Power

## Empty We Came, Empty We Will All Leave
We entered this world with nothing, and we cannot carry its possessions beyond the grave. Wealth, applause, influence and achievements may fill our days, but they cannot purchase eternity. “Dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return” (Genesis 3:19, KJV). Job declared, “Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither” (Job 1:21). What shall it profit a person to gain the whole world and lose their soul? (Mark 8:36). Today, turn from sin and seek the Lord Jesus Christ, whose salvation is greater than anything this passing world can offer.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## Humanity Truly Needs God
Nations advance in science, medicine, technology and architecture, yet human wisdom cannot remove our need for God. Disasters can remind us how fragile even our greatest achievements are. Every gift, talent and opportunity calls for humility and gratitude, not pride. Like a child who forgets the parent who nurtured them, humanity can forget the source of life itself. “For in him we live, and move, and have our being” (Acts 17:28). Do not exchange integrity for shortcuts or compromise your conscience for quick rewards. “The blessing of the LORD, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it” (Proverbs 10:22). Even when others turn away, choose faithfulness, compassion and holiness. Humanity needs repentance, the grace of God, and peace through Jesus Christ.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## Revival of Repentance, Salvation and Holiness
True revival begins with hearts returning to God. It is more than a gathering, emotion or outward appearance: it produces repentance, forgiveness, love, obedience and transformed living. “Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out” (Acts 3:19). The Lord calls us not merely to hear His Word, but to live it. Come to Jesus with sincerity and let His grace lead you into a holy life.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## Knowing God for Yourself and Understanding the Scriptures Is Now
This is the time to know God personally, not merely through your parents, pastor, church or denomination. Daniel 11:32 says, “the people that do know their God shall be strong, and do exploits.” Search the Scriptures, pray, test teachings carefully, and grow in your own relationship with Christ. “Study to shew thyself approved unto God” (2 Timothy 2:15). A personal, Scripture-grounded faith helps you recognize misleading teachings and remain steadfast.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## What Are Wealth, Technology, High Position and the Good Things of Life Without Jesus?
Success and innovation can serve people well, but none can reconcile us to God. Life's good gifts are not substitutes for the Giver. Jesus asks, “For what shall it profit a man, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul?” (Mark 8:36). Let your achievements become opportunities to honor God and serve others. Receive the greater gift of salvation through Christ.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## Until You Make Peace with Jesus
Titles, reputation and accomplishments cannot give us the reconciliation with God that Christ offers. “Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ” (Romans 5:1). Your life matters, and you are invited to receive forgiveness, a renewed purpose and hope beyond this present world. Do not postpone your response to God's grace.

**Call to salvation: I SAY YES TO JESUS**

## Salvation Response — I Say Yes to Jesus
Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). Scripture calls us to repent and believe the gospel (Mark 1:15), and promises that those who call upon the Lord shall be saved (Romans 10:9–13). Salvation is God's gift of grace, received through faith (Ephesians 2:8–9).

**Suggested prayer:** Lord Jesus Christ, I acknowledge my sins and my need for Your mercy. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. Forgive me, lead me in repentance, and teach me to follow You in faith and holiness. Amen.

A prayer is not a magic formula. Encourage visitors to trust in Christ, read the Bible, pray, connect with a faithful Christian fellowship, and continue growing in love and obedience.
