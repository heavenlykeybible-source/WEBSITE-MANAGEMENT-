# Strength for Today — Doctrinal Review Standard (provisional)

## Authority order
1. Scripture, accurately quoted in KJV and interpreted in context.
2. **Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement's actual approved Statement of Faith** — NOT YET PROVIDED OR VERIFIED IN FULL. Do not claim clause-by-clause compliance before obtaining it.
3. Official Deeper Christian Life Ministry doctrinal statement: https://dclm.org/about/what-we-believe/
4. Official Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries beliefs: https://mountainoffire.org/About/Beliefs
5. Pastor W. F. Kumuyi's official sermons and DCLM Daily Manna: https://dclm.org/about/w-f-kumuyi/ and https://dailymanna.dclm.org — reference for themes, NOT content to copy.
6. MFM devotional reference to be identified from a verified official source; do not assume Daily Manna is its title or use unverified republishers.

## Non-negotiable editorial checks per day
- Preserve original order: date/topic; main KJV and supporting texts; brief opening story; short reflection; brief Bible story; its reflection; unique sourced worldwide historical story; its reflection; challenge; prayers; Heavenly Key footer.
- Repentance, salvation by Christ, holiness, revival, spiritual maturity, evangelism, destiny and faithful living are the overarching mission.
- Review the whole canon across five years and practical life spheres: family, education, employment, finance, public service, leadership, relationships, care for the vulnerable, and missions.
- Verify exact verse bounds, quote text, contextual meaning, references and doctrinal claims. Distinguish source quotations from commentary.
- Review for genuine repentance, restitution where appropriate, Christ-centred salvation, grace and holy conduct; no manipulation, false certainty, fear-based speculation or fabricated testimony.
- Check all real-world accounts for verifiable facts and non-repetition; check Bible stories for canonical source and relevance.
- Where DCLM and MFM emphases differ, flag for Heavenly Key owner review. Do not silently treat all three ministries as doctrinally identical.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted Daily Manna, sermons or other devotional prose.
- Do not mark publication_ready until every day is inspected and the approved Heavenly Key faith statement is obtained.

## Review classifications
PASS (supported by Scripture and approved ministry statement); REPAIR (inaccurate quote/fact or inconsistent presentation); OWNER_REVIEW (genuine doctrinal ambiguity or inter-ministry difference); SOURCE_NEEDED (historical evidence or official ministry statement unavailable); BLOCK (fabricated story, doctrinal contradiction, or unverified publication claim).

## State of completion
This file establishes the standards. It is **not** evidence that all 1,826 daily records have been scrutinized or repaired. The existing Revision 50 GitHub manuscript and Revision 52 audit remain the working baseline.
