# Revision 52 — Original daily structure audit

Audited 1,826 days of preserved Revision 51 source for the exact owner's original presentation order:

1. Date/topic
2. Main KJV and supporting Scripture texts
3. Short opening story
4. Short opening write-up
5. Short Bible story
6. Short Bible-story spiritual lesson
7. Sourced historical story from around the world
8. Short historical-story spiritual lesson
9. Daily challenge
10. Three prayers
11. Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement footer

## Structural results
- All 1,826 days lack **separate named** opening-story, opening-reflection, after-Bible-story reflection, and after-world-story reflection fields. Some of these elements may be embedded in the long devotional message; they need extraction and editing rather than assuming they are wholly absent.
- 1,806 days lack a separate world-story application field.
- 1,441 days lack historical-story titles.
- 40 days lack historical-story narrative text.
- 14 days lack historical source URLs.
- 34 days lack Bible-story titles.
- Two days do not have exactly two supporting passage objects.
- No day is publication approved.

## Critical preservation
This audit used the Revision 51 source archive (which descends from Revision 49) and **does not include the 1,750 newly filled supporting quotations in Revision 50**. Do not overwrite the 11 canonical Revision 50 GitHub JSONL chunks with this older-source material.

## Mission and quality gates
All daily entries must retain the ministry's repentance, salvation, revival, holiness, destiny fulfilment, and practical Christian living focus across family, study, work, leadership and service. A world-history story must be unique and individually sourced, not an invented or renamed repetition. Confirm the Bible story against its reference and check all KJV texts before publication.
